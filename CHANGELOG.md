# Changelog (simulated)

Versions and contents below are invented for the demo; see README.

## 8.23.0
- Design Controls: impact preview. Before a design change is approved, see the DHF outputs, risk controls and cleared devices it touches, and whether a Special 510(k) is likely.

## 8.22.0
- Design Controls: a design change dashboard shows every open change with its DHF and risk-file trace and open re-verification, so quality sees what blocks the next Special 510(k).

## 8.21.0
- Design Controls: a pre-submission check for Special 510(k)s lists exactly what is missing from a design change record, from change request through DHF and risk file to closed re-verification.

## 8.20.0
- Design Controls: Special 510(k) pack. For a design change to a cleared device, the design-control evidence FDA reviews is assembled from the change record, its DHF and risk-file trace and its re-verification, with nothing traced by hand.

## 8.19.0
- Design Controls: one design change log per product, each change with its DHF and risk-file trace and its re-verification, ready for an audit or a Special 510(k).

## 8.18.0
- Change Management: approving a design change now opens the re-verification it requires, traced from the design history file and risk file, and the change cannot close until every task is done.

## 8.17.0
- Design Controls: every design change is traced automatically from the change request through the design history file to the risk file, with what must be re-verified.

## 8.16.0
- Design Controls: every design change is linked to the risk assessments and design history file entries it affects.

## 8.15.0
- Labelling: label changes follow their own controlled approval route (regulatory review, artwork proof, approval), separate from ordinary documents.

## 8.14.0
- Software Validation: every software release carries its IEC 62304 safety class, validation evidence and e-signed approval inside the change record.

## 8.13.0
- Change Management: change impact analysis. Every change request shows the design history file outputs and risk controls it touches, in one place.

## 8.12.0
- Learning Management: a design or process change that makes a document effective assigns retraining to every role that uses it; the change cannot close until training is complete.

## 8.11.0
- e-Signatures: Part 11 audit trail export (who signed, meaning, UTC time) for auditors.

## 8.10.0
- Change Management: validated software change workflow (IEC 62304 impact assessment, validation evidence, e-signed approval), traced into the design history file.

## 8.9.0
- Document holders: mass licensing and mass approval.
- Licensee logins expire on schedule.
- Security: verbose event logging.
